The first thing worth checking on the AquaSculpt page is the plain description of the product: what it is, what goes into it and who it is meant for. Everything else on the page, from pricing to reviews, makes more sense once those points are clear.

Next, read the buying terms. Check the price per unit and not only the bundle price, whether shipping is extra, how long delivery takes to your address, and whether the order is a single purchase or a repeating subscription. These details are normally shown next to the order buttons or in the small print below them.

Finally, find the refund policy and the contact details. Note how many days the guarantee runs, whether opened items can be returned, who pays return shipping, and which email or phone number handles requests. Saving a copy of the order confirmation makes any later claim much simpler.

Buying from the official page, and not from a marketplace listing or a copy site, matters for two reasons. It is the only place where the seller's own guarantee applies, and it is the surest way to get the current formula and the current price.

Customer comments on a seller's own page are chosen by the seller, so read them as examples and not as proof. Results differ from person to person. The parts of the page that can be checked, such as the label, the price, the guarantee and the contact details, are a firmer basis for a decision than any single story.

Read the full ingredient list and directions on the page, and speak with a doctor first if you take medication or have a health condition.
